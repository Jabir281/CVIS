Institutional Mission

Computational Synthesis of Reality

Founded within the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at BRAC University, CVIS focuses on fundamental algorithms that bridge the gap between continuous physical optics and discrete neural decision making.

01

Sensor Photometry

Capturing multi-view spectral photons and radiometric data with calibrated hardware genlocks and low optical distortion.

02

Spatial Depth Alignment

Formulating structured point clouds, dense occupancy grids, and real-time metric depth estimation on edge computing rigs.

03

Volumetric Representations

Extracting equivariant latent representations via 3D tiled convolutional networks and continuous radiance field functions.

04

Clinical & Applied Inference

Validating algorithms across high-stakes benchmarks including clinical neuroimaging datasets and autonomous robotics tasks.
